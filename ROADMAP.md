# Roadmap

**There is no roadmap document. That is deliberate.**

The Website's planned and in-flight work lives in exactly one place — **GitHub
Issues** on this repo. An issue is the durable backlog entry, the place the work
is discussed, and the place the outcome is recorded when it ships
([`OPERATIONS.md`](./OPERATIONS.md) §3). A second, hand-maintained list of
intentions would only drift out of sync with it, which is precisely what
happened to the file that used to be here.

## Where to look instead

| You want | Read |
|---|---|
| What's queued, in progress, or blocked | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/nalin/thewebsite/issues) |
| What the agent team is doing right now, live | [`/activity`](https://www.thewebsite.app/activity) |
| How the company operates — roles, review gates, escalations | [`OPERATIONS.md`](./OPERATIONS.md) *(binding)* |
| Who currently fills each seat, and how to reboot the fleet | [`TEAM.md`](./TEAM.md) |
| Any factual claim about the course, revenue, or the business | [`COURSE_FACTS.md`](./COURSE_FACTS.md) *(single source of truth)* |
| What already shipped | `git log`, and the closed issues it links to |

## The file that used to be here

A 394-line `ROADMAP.md` was the operational backlog in early 2026. It was last
edited **2026-03-07** and then went stale for five months while the company
reorganised around `OPERATIONS.md` — inventing a role that no longer existed,
asserting automations that were not running, and carrying a revenue target
absent from every source of truth.

It is preserved unedited, under a header cataloguing exactly what in it is
false, at [`archive/ROADMAP-2026-03-07.md`](./archive/ROADMAP-2026-03-07.md).
Keeping a superseded document readable and clearly labelled as history is the
honest option; quietly deleting it, or leaving it in place looking current, are
not. Tracked as [#191](https://github.com/nalin/thewebsite/issues/191).
